Privacy Policy

Digital Fuel Gauge (Android)
Last updated: August 18, 2026

This policy describes how the Android app Digital Fuel Gauge (“the App”) handles information. The App is a local fuel and trip tracker. It does not require an account and does not operate its own servers.

Short version: Vehicle details, fuel logs, and trip distances stay on your device. Location is used only while you run a GPS trip, to measure distance. Coordinates are not saved. The App has no ads and does not sell your data.

Information we store on your device

You provide this information, and it is saved in the App’s local storage:

  • Vehicle names and settings (consumption, tank capacity)
  • Odometer readings and estimated remaining fuel
  • Fuel-up history (date, odometer, liters added, remaining fuel, full-tank flag)
  • Distance from a GPS trip (kilometers only), used to update the odometer

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, or payment information.

Location

The App requests precise and approximate location so you can optionally track a trip with GPS. Location is used only to calculate how far you have driven during an active trip.

  • Tracking starts only when you turn it on.
  • While a trip is active, a foreground notification is shown and location is read in the foreground.
  • The App does not request background location when a trip is not running.
  • Raw coordinates are processed in memory and are not stored. Only the resulting trip distance may be saved on the device.
  • You can deny location permission. Fuel logging and remaining-fuel estimates still work without GPS.

Location is obtained through Google Play services’ location APIs on your device. Google’s handling of that data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Notifications

On Android 13 and later, the App may ask for notification permission so it can show the ongoing GPS trip notification. Notifications are not used for marketing.

How information is used

On-device data is used only to:

  • Estimate remaining fuel and driving range
  • Keep a fuel history for each vehicle
  • Add GPS trip distance to your odometer when you end a trip

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your App data with third parties for advertising or analytics. The App does not include ads or analytics SDKs.

Information may leave the App only in these cases:

  • Device backup. Android backup (if enabled on your device) may include App storage in your Google account backup.
  • GasBuddy (optional). If you open nearby stations, the App launches the GasBuddy app or website. The App does not send your location, vehicles, or fuel logs to GasBuddy. GasBuddy is a separate service with its own privacy policy.
  • Legal requests. We have no user database to produce. Any disclosure would be limited to what the law requires of us as the developer.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete it. You can remove it by clearing the App’s storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the App.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Data is stored in the App’s private storage on your device. We do not operate a cloud account for the App. No method of storage is completely secure; keep your device locked and updated.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the “Last updated” date on this page. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
